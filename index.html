INSIDE THE ATELIER

How we handcraft each bloom

01

The wire core

Every stem begins as a single length of pliable craft wire, cut and gauged by hand in our Jaipur studio.

02

The velvet weave

Chenille fibres are wound petal by petal, layered until the bloom catches light like real velvet.

03

Shaping & posing

Petals are curled, stems bent and balanced — so your flower holds any pose you give it.

04

The final finish

Each piece is inspected, gently steamed, and wrapped in tissue before it ever leaves the studio.
