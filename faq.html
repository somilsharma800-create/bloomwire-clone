FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BloomWire's handcrafted blooms.

What are BloomWire flowers made of?
Our flowers are handcrafted from chenille wire (also known as pipe cleaners). Each petal is wound by hand from soft chenille fibre, which gives them their velvet-like texture. The stems use a flexible craft-grade wire core that bends and holds any pose.
Do they look realistic?
Yes! Our chenille flowers are designed to mimic real blooms. The velvet texture catches light like real petals, and many customers tell us people can't believe they aren't real flowers at first glance.
How long do they last?
Forever! That's the beauty of chenille flowers — no water, no wilting, no fading. Your bloom will look the same in year five as it does on day one. Just dust gently with a soft brush occasionally.
What is the shipping policy?
We offer FREE shipping on all orders above ₹499. Orders below ₹499 have a flat ₹49 shipping fee. All orders are shipped within 2-3 business days via standard delivery across India (3-5 business days delivery time).
What is the return policy?
We offer 7-day easy returns. If you're not happy with your bloom, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange a return or exchange. Custom orders are non-returnable.
Can I bend and pose the stems?
Absolutely! The wire core inside each stem is designed to be poseable. You can bend, curl, and arrange your flowers however you like — they'll hold the shape you give them.
Are they safe for allergy sufferers?
Yes! Many of our customers buy specifically because they can't keep real flowers due to allergies. Chenille flowers produce no pollen and trigger no allergic reactions.
Do you offer wholesale pricing?
Yes! We offer wholesale pricing for bulk orders, events, and retail partnerships. Visit our Wholesale page for details.
What are Petals Rewards?
Petals are our loyalty points. You earn Petals on every order (20 Petals per ₹100), reviews (+50), Instagram tags (+25), referrals (+100), and on your birthday (+100). Redeem them for discounts, free keychains, and free blooms. Learn more.
Can I gift wrap my order?
Yes! All orders come tissue-wrapped by default. You can add a handwritten gift card message at checkout, and we also offer premium gift wrapping for an additional fee.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently we ship within India only. We're working on international shipping — follow us on Instagram for updates!
