OUR STORY

Real flowers fade. Memories shouldn't.

We are a newly launched artisan studio crafting everlasting wire & velvet chenille floral sculptures — permanent keepsakes made to stay beautiful long after natural blooms fade.

BloomWire began in a small Jaipur studio with a simple idea: that flowers could be beautiful and permanent. We handcraft each bloom from chenille wire — also known as pipe cleaners — winding every petal by hand until it catches light like real velvet.

What started as a single peony stem has grown into a full collection of single flowers, bouquets, pots, and keychains — each one made to be kept, gifted, and treasured for years. No water, no wilting, no fading.

We believe in the quiet luxury of things made by hand. Every stem is unique, every petal is individual, and every bloom carries the story of the artisan who shaped it. That's the BloomWire promise: flowers that never fade, made by people who care.

Our Process

01

The wire core

Every stem begins as a single length of pliable craft wire, cut and gauged by hand in our Jaipur studio.

02

The velvet weave

Chenille fibres are wound petal by petal, layered until the bloom catches light like real velvet.

03

Shaping & posing

Petals are curled, stems bent and balanced — so your flower holds any pose you give it.

04

The final finish

Each piece is inspected, gently steamed, and wrapped in tissue before it ever leaves the studio.
